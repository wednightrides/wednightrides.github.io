We made a zine!

It's an "atlas of unforgettable Philly bike rides" featuring 10 of our rides from the last five years. Beautifully designed and printed by [Inner Loop Press](https://innerloop.press), featuring art from several of our talented riders, this zine documents a handful of fabulous rides and serves as a memento and a small but mighty work of art and craft.

![WNR Zine]({{site.baseurl}}/assets/images/zine.jpg)

The zine is a limited edition! We'll have them for sale at the zine launch at [Partners & Son](https://partnersandson.com/) from 6-7p on Wed Sep 30 before the next ride, and after that they'll be available for sale at selected bookshops and bike shops, but if you want to be sure you get on one, pre-order below, and then pay either cash or Venmo (@Stefan-Zajic).

The zine costs $5. This is not a fundraiser, we just wanted to make this and share it with the world. But if, after paying Inner Loop Press for materials, printing, and design, we have money left over, we'll donate the extra to local bike orgs (split between [BCGP](https://bicyclecoalition.org), [PBA](https://bikeaction.org), and [Neighborhood Bike Works](https://neighborhoodbikeworks.org)).

Pre-order [here](https://forms.gle/gNAdxsyRE88T4Apa6)!
